# Areas and bosses

Each area lasts ten minutes and contains unique enemies, mini-bosses, and a final boss.

| # | Area | Boss |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Burning Plains | Fire Phoenix |
| 2 | Frozen Wastes | Ice Yeti |
| 3 | Thunder Peaks | Storm Dragon |
| 4 | Poison Marsh | Giant Scorpion |
| 5 | Shadow Realm | Necrotic Bat King |
| 6 | Celestial Temple | Fallen Angel |

## Boss move sets

- **Fire Phoenix:** Fire Dash, Feather Barrage, Flame Tornado, Meteor Rain, Rebirth.
- **Earth Golem:** Boulder Leap, Ground Spike, Quicksand, Rock Fall, Boulder Bounce.
- **Water Leviathan:** Wave Dash, Wave Crash, Whirlpool, Rain Clouds, Bubble Beam.
- **Wind Roc:** Sky Dive, Razor Gust, Tornado Trail, Wing Cyclone.
- **Ice Yeti:** Snowball, Ice Shards, Blizzard, Frozen Slam.
- **Storm Dragon:** Lightning Strike, Thunder Roar, Lightning Dash, Thunder Dash.
- **Giant Scorpion:** Venom Tail, Toxic Glob, Poison Stinger, Acid Spray.
- **Necrotic Bat King:** not designed.
- **Fallen Angel:** not designed.

> [!warning] Scope mismatch
> Earth Golem and Wind Roc have move sets, but neither appears in the current six-area sequence. Decide whether they are challenge bosses, mini-bosses, or missing areas.

## Enemy archetypes

- Flying enemy
- Fast enemy
- Slow charging enemy
- Ranged enemy
- Death-explosion enemy
