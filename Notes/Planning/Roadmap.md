# Roadmap

## Demo target

- Four complete spirits: Fire, Wind, Water, and Earth.
- Two complete areas: Burning Plains and Earth Plains (working title).
- Two final bosses: Fire Phoenix and Earth Golem.
- Complete menu, selection, gameplay, upgrade, save, victory, defeat, and return-to-menu flow.

The complete acceptance criteria are in [[Notes/Planning/Demo Scope and Completion Plan|Demo scope and completion plan]].

## Milestone 1 — Burning Plains vertical slice

- [ ] First build a two-to-three-minute development loop, then expand it to ten minutes after validation.
- [ ] Complete one playable loop: menu → Fire Spirit selection → Burning Plains → Fire Phoenix → reward → menu.
- [ ] Connect victory, loss, retry, pause, and return-to-menu flows.
- [ ] Validate player movement and stationary/moving attack modes.
- [ ] Complete Fire Spirit weapon and three abilities.
- [ ] Add core enemy archetypes, experience, level-up choices, and boss timer.
- [ ] Establish baseline balance values and repeatable playtest and performance checklists.
- [ ] Profile 50, 100, 200, and 250 enemies in a Development Build.
- [ ] Exclude development-only DLLs and unused runtime assets from player builds.

The detailed, prioritized checklist is in [[Notes/Planning/Project Audit and Backlog|Project audit and backlog]].

## Milestone 2 — Four complete demo spirits

- [ ] Complete Fire: Flame Bow, Fiery Feathers, Fiery Talons, and Phoenix Dive.
- [ ] Complete Wind: Chakrams, Razor Wind, Tornado, and Gale Barrier.
- [ ] Complete Water: Water Trident, Tidal Wave, Whirlpool, and Rain Clouds.
- [ ] Complete Earth: Stone Hammer, Quicksand Domain, Boulder Throw, and Stone Spikes.
- [ ] Complete final spirit art, animation, weapon art, ability VFX, icons, descriptions, audio, and upgrade paths.
- [ ] Validate all four spirits as starter, main, and support spirits.

## Milestone 3 — Earth Plains

- [ ] Confirm the final Earth-area name and visual identity.
- [ ] Create the Earth area scene, environment art, collision, hazards, and spawn data.
- [ ] Create at least three Earth enemy archetypes and an elite variation.
- [ ] Complete the Earth Golem boss and its five planned attacks.
- [ ] Add Earth Plains unlock, victory reward, and demo-complete presentation.

## Milestone 4 — Demo release pass

- [ ] Add a small versioned save system for settings and demo unlocks.
- [ ] Complete keyboard and controller support.
- [ ] Complete essential settings and accessibility options.
- [ ] Add music, ambience, SFX, credits, licenses, and feedback information.
- [ ] Meet the performance target in both demo areas.
- [ ] Remove placeholder assets, missing references, debug tools, and development-only build content.
- [ ] Run external playtests and fix all completion blockers.
- [ ] Produce the final build, screenshots, trailer, description, and instructions.

## After the demo — system depth

- [ ] Implement three-spirit acquisition and slot rotation.
- [ ] Complete ability leveling and upgrade presentation.
- [ ] Define and prototype the fusion system.
- [ ] Build reusable area, wave, mini-boss, and boss data.
- [ ] Add save data and permanent unlock progression.

## After the demo — content production

- [ ] Build and tune all six areas.
- [ ] Complete remaining spirits and bosses.
- [ ] Add audio, visual effects, accessibility, settings, and onboarding.
- [ ] Run performance, balance, and release-readiness passes.

## Design gaps

- [ ] Meta-progression economy and unlock pacing.
- [ ] Fusion recipes and eligibility rules.
- [ ] Necrotic Spirit abilities.
- [ ] Holy Spirit detailed abilities.
- [ ] Ice Crystal upgrade path.
- [ ] RockFall identity.
- [ ] Long-term campaign role for Wind Roc after Earth Golem becomes the demo's second boss.
