[Heading 2] Game Level Design
[Normal] The game consists of 6 different themed areas.
[Normal] Each area lasts 10 minutes
[Normal] Each area contains unique enemies, mini-bosses, and a final boss
[Normal] Total playtime: approximately 60 minutes
[Heading 2] Game Lore
[Normal] Long ago, the Spirit World and Human World existed in harmony.
[Normal] A powerful corruption known as The Void began consuming spirits and twisting them into dangerous creatures known as the Elemental Kin.
[Normal] Spirit Tamers are warriors capable of forming contracts with powerful elemental spirits and channeling their power through magical weapons.
[Normal] The player must travel across six corrupted regions, purify the elemental guardians, and restore balance between the two worlds.

[Normal] Starting Spirit System
[Normal] The player begins with:
[Normal] Fire Spirit (unlocked by default)
[Normal] Other spirits are unlocked through special summoning challenges.
[Normal] Example Progression
[Normal] Defeat the Fire Phoenix → Unlock Ice Challenge
[Normal] Defeat the Ice Yeti → Unlock Lightning Challenge
[Normal] Continue unlocking spirits through gameplay
[Normal] This system rewards players for progression rather than relying on random summons or gambling mechanics.
[Normal] Leveling System
[Normal] Whenever the player levels up, the game randomly offers upgrades for spirit abilities, player stats, or new spirit contracts. Upgrade cards have no minimum player-level requirement; valid cards can appear from the beginning of a run. Spirit ownership, ability order, maximum ranks, and other functional prerequisites still apply.
[Normal] The player can have a maximum of three spirits during a run:
[Normal] One starting spirit
[Normal] Up to two additional spirits acquired during the run
[Normal] Fusion System
[Normal] When two spirits reach the maximum level of a specific ability, one of the available upgrade choices may become a Fusion Ability.
[Normal] Fusion abilities are based on the combination of the two spirits and provide powerful new attacks or effects.

[Heading 2] Current Gameplay Details
[Detail Note] Design snapshot: The current game structure combines automatic combat, movement-based spirit behavior, and build choices across a ten-minute area run.
[Heading 3] Run Rules
[List Paragraph] A run supports up to three contracted spirits: one starting spirit and up to two spirits acquired through level-up choices.
[List Paragraph] The active main spirit can be changed during play, allowing the player to switch weapon forms and combat roles.
[List Paragraph] While the player is stationary, the main spirit becomes its elemental weapon and attacks automatically.
[List Paragraph] While the player is moving, the main spirit casts abilities like a support spirit; support spirits continue attacking in both movement states.
[List Paragraph] Each area lasts ten minutes and ends with a boss encounter.
[Heading 3] Spirit Roster at a Glance
[Heading 3] Area Progression
[Normal] The six-area sequence currently moves through Burning Plains, Frozen Wastes, Thunder Peaks, Poison Marsh, Shadow Realm, and Celestial Temple. Their final bosses are the Fire Phoenix, Ice Yeti, Storm Dragon, Giant Scorpion, Necrotic Bat King, and Fallen Angel.
[Heading 3] Enemy Roles
[Heading 3] Recently Implemented Systems
[List Paragraph] Upgrade offers are randomized without minimum player-level gates.
[List Paragraph] Wind Tornadoes spawn in a random ring 5-9 units from the player, damage nearby enemies over time, and steadily pull enemies toward their center.
[List Paragraph] Gale Barrier creates a visible expanding wind pulse, shields the player, and damages and pushes nearby enemies away.
[List Paragraph] Wind Chakrams behave as boomerangs: they damage enemies while travelling outward, return to the player, and can damage the same enemy again on the return path.
[List Paragraph] Flying enemies pressure positioning and can approach over obstacles.
[List Paragraph] Fast enemies pursue aggressively, while slow chargers telegraph heavier attacks.
[List Paragraph] Ranged enemies force the player to keep moving and manage projectile space.
[List Paragraph] Death-explosion enemies create temporary danger zones after defeat.

[Heading 4] Weapon Behavior
[Normal] The Roc throws wind-forged chakrams toward nearby enemies. Each chakram spins outward, travels up to seven units, then homes back to the player and disappears when caught. An enemy can be hit once on the outward trip and once again on the return trip. Damage, attack speed, projectile speed, projectile size, and multishot upgrades affect the weapon.

[List Paragraph] Lv3: 14 damage, longer targeting range, and faster flight.

[List Paragraph] Lv5: 23 damage and maximum base throw speed.

[Heading 1] Player Character
[Heading 2] Description
[Normal] The player takes on the role of a Spirit Tamer. Spirit Tamers form contracts with powerful elemental spirits and channel their powers through enchanted crystals.
[Normal] Each spirit grants unique attacks, abilities, and passive bonuses that allow the player to create different combat builds throughout a run.
[Heading 2] Combat System
[Heading 3] Weapon – Spirit Gauntlet
[Normal] The Spirit Gauntlet is a magical glove containing a central gemstone used to summon and channel elemental spirits. Spirits channelled through the main gemstone manifest as powerful elemental weapons, each unique to its corresponding spirit.
[Heading 3] Attacking
[Normal] The player has two methods of attacking.
[Heading 4] Method 1 – Primary Attack
[Normal] Condition: The player must be standing still.
[Normal] Description:
While standing still, the player channels their Primary Spirit through the Spirit Gauntlet, transforming it into that spirit's elemental weapon. The player then attacks automatically with this weapon until they begin moving.
[Heading 4] Method 2 – Spirit Attack
[Normal] Condition: The player must be moving.
[Normal] Description:
While moving, the Primary Spirit stops using its weapon form and instead fights like a support spirit, automatically casting its unique abilities. Any additional contracted spirits also continue using their abilities, allowing the player to focus on movement and dodging enemy attacks.
